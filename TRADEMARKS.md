# MCV Trademark Policy

The source license and the brand are separate.

`MCV`, `MCV Browser`, the MCV dot-matrix wordmark, application icon, product
trade dress and associated F Corp marks identify the official project. No
software license grants permission to use those marks in a way that suggests
an unofficial build, fork, service or company is produced, sponsored or
endorsed by F Corp.

## Allowed without separate permission

- truthful statements such as “based on MCV Browser”;
- links to the official repository;
- screenshots in reviews, documentation and educational material;
- unmodified redistribution permitted by the source license, clearly identified
  as an unofficial mirror when it is not distributed by F Corp.

## Permission required

- using MCV marks in the name or icon of a modified build;
- presenting a fork as the official MCV Browser;
- using confusingly similar names, logos or visual identity;
- using MCV marks to advertise a paid product, hosted service or commercial
  support offering;
- claiming partnership, certification or endorsement.

Forks must use a distinct name and identity. Removing the marks does not grant
commercial rights to the code: commercial use remains governed by
[`COMMERCIAL.md`](COMMERCIAL.md) and a separate written agreement.

Descriptive fair use and other rights provided by applicable law are not
restricted by this policy.
